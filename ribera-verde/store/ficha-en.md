## Full description

<<<
Your aunt Maite left you her flat in Ribera Verde, a working-class neighbourhood by the estuary: her grow tents, her strain diary… and a €30,000 debt owed to people who don't wait.

GROW
Set up your first tent in your aunt's flat and work your way up to a whole warehouse. Lights, pots, watering, nutrients, room climate, pests and a timer on every tent. Each strain grows its own way: some short and bushy, others tall and lanky.

CROSS AND STABILISE
Landraces from around the world and dozens of named crosses. Take an F1 to a stable F4 line, hunt star phenotypes, keep mothers and take cuttings. Your aunt's diary holds the recipe for each one, and the Gene Bank keeps everything you find.

SELL AND SURVIVE
Neighbourhood customers, clubs, the fish market and bulk buyers. Meanwhile: police patrols, thieves, rising heat, raids and debt deadlines. Launder your money through your own businesses, win people over and always have a plan to stay out of trouble.

A WHOLE REGION
From the farmhouse in Mendialde to the shipyards, from the old town of Ibarbide to the port. Bus, bike, scooter or your own car. A calendar with seasons, weather and club nights.

· Original, hand-drawn pixel art
· Original music for each area, by day and by night
· Touch controls built for landscape play with your thumbs
· In Spanish and English
· Plays offline, no ads
· Chapters 1 to 3 free; the full game is a single purchase (no pay-to-win)
· Autosave and backup

NOTICE: Landrace Hunters is a work of fiction for adults (18+). It is not a growing guide and does not encourage using or selling drugs: growing and selling cannabis is a crime in many countries. All characters, places, brands and strains in the game are fictional.
>>>
